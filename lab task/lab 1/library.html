About the Library

The COMSATS Library Information Services acts as a catalyst for learning, research, and scholarly pursuits. With a vast collection of printed volumes, international research journals, and extensive digital repositories, the library empowers students and faculty to excel in cutting-edge computing, engineering, and scientific disciplines.

“A library is not a luxury but one of the necessities of life. It connects curious minds to endless horizons of human knowledge.”

Students are encouraged to maintain quiet study decorum at all times. Please note that timely return of borrowed books ensures that resources remain accessible to the entire academic community.

Library Facilities & Services

The library provides comprehensive learning support, including:

  • High-Speed Digital Lab: Equipped with modern PCs for academic research and paper retrieval.
  • HEC National Digital Library Access: Over 75,000 peer-reviewed e-books and thousands of journals.
  • Quiet Study & Discussion Pods: Dedicated spaces for individual revision and collaborative group studies.
  • Reference & Citation Assistance: Professional librarians available to assist with IEEE/APA referencing.
  • Photocopying & Printing Station: Affordable document printing and scanning services for students.

Book Borrowing Procedure

Follow these sequential steps to issue books from the circulation desk:

  1. Search for the desired title or call number using the library Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC).
  2. Locate the book in the designated subject shelf stacks using the shelf classification code.
  3. Bring the selected book along with your valid COMSATS Student ID Card to the circulation counter.
  4. Wait for the staff to scan the barcode and stamp the due date on the slip inside the back cover.
  5. Keep the physical receipt and ensure the book is returned or renewed within 14 days to avoid late fines.
